From raw file to finished report

Six steps, one upload. You can edit the results at any step.

  1. 1

    Upload your data

    Bring in a CSV, Excel, or JSON file. That's all Cascade needs.

  2. 2

    Clean it automatically

    Cascade removes duplicates and blank rows, fixes spacing and capitalization, and standardizes mixed date formats. A report lists every change.

  3. 3

    Build charts

    Pick the columns and chart type that tell the story best.

  4. 4

    Get an insight for each chart

    Every chart comes with a written insight. Edit it to sharpen it or add context only you know.

  5. 5

    Review the summary and recommendations

    Get a summary of the whole dataset and a set of actions to take, based on everything Cascade found.

  6. 6

    Export a PDF

    Combine the charts, insights, summary, and recommendations into one report you can share.

Recommendations from the same report

Net revenue by sales_channel 'Online' makes up 41% of the total 'net_revenue'. Look into what drives this concentration and whether relying so heavily on one sales_channel value is a risk. Bars show totals, but groups have very different numbers of records (510 to 2,058). Compare the average 'net_revenue' per record too before deciding which 'sales_channel' performs best.
Profit ÷ net_revenue × 100 by discount_% The widest difference in this chart is between '0%' (47.70%) and '20%' (33.80%). Find out what drives that gap before acting on the average (41.11%).
